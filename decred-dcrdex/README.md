# Bison Wallet TEST on Umbrel

Bison Wallet is a multi-coin crypto wallet with a built-in decentralized
exchange. You can trade Bitcoin, Ethereum, Decred and more, directly
from your own Umbrel — no account signup, no trading fees, and your
coins never leave your device.

This is the **TEST** version of the app. It is meant for trying things
out and may change or break at any time — please don't keep large
amounts of funds in it.

## How to install

You only need your Umbrel and a couple of minutes.

1. Open your Umbrel in the browser and go to the **App Store**.
2. Click the three dots (**⋯**) in the top-right corner and choose
   **Community App Stores**.
3. Copy this address, paste it into the box, and click **Add**:

   ```text
   https://github.com/peterzen/dcrdex
   ```

4. A new store called **Bison Wallet TEST** appears in the list. Click
   **Open** next to it.
5. Find **Bison Wallet TEST** and click **Install**.
6. That's it! When the install finishes, the app shows up on your
   Umbrel home screen. Open it and follow the on-screen steps to set up
   your wallet — you'll be asked to choose a password first.

## Good to know

- Your wallet data stays on your Umbrel. Write down your seed phrase
  during setup and keep it somewhere safe — it's the only way to
  recover your wallet.
- Updates appear in the umbrelOS App Store like for any other app.
- Questions or problems? Please open an issue at
  <https://github.com/peterzen/dcrdex/issues>.
